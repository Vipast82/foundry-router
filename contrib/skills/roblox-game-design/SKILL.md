# Game design for Roblox

## Loops
Three layers must each work: the core loop (moment to moment), the meta game (hour to hour) and long-term progression (day to day). If the raw core actions are not fun, nothing above them survives: prototype and playtest the core first.

## First session
- Players decide fast; many never return after the first minute. Spawn into the world (no long logo or cutscene), do the core action within seconds, land a first reward early, and open the next goal before minute one ends.
- Teach through play: the environment, highlighted next actions and short, skippable prompts; one mechanic, one button, one goal at a time.

## Levels and pacing
Safe introduction -> development -> twist -> conclusion. Guide with light, colour, landmarks and reward placement rather than text. Failures should feel like the player's mistake, never the game's.

## Economy
Every currency source needs a sink; prices scale with the power curve; watch hoarding, devaluation and churn at choke points. Instrument sources and sinks from day one.

## Retention
Early days: understandable, novel, stable. Then progression, mastery and reasons to return (collections, goals, events). Later: social comparison, live events, community. Serve several player motivations (achievers, explorers, socializers, competitors); over-serving one drives the others away.

## Ethics (always, and especially for kids)
- No dark patterns: no fake scarcity or countdowns, no confusing currency conversions to hide prices, no pressure loops that punish stopping, no pay-to-win against other players.
- Random rewards: show the odds, follow Roblox policy on paid random items, add a pity mechanism, and keep an earnable path.
- Streaks forgive a missed day; notifications and return triggers respect the player's time.
- Price points, paid features and anything targeting spending are owner decisions.

## Using the references
`player-psychology.md` and `growth.md` include persuasion techniques (charm pricing, expiring boosts, timers). Use them to understand and measure behaviour; apply them only within the ethics rules above and the owner's direction.
